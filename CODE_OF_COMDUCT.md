# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want srTokens to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background, experience level, identity or location.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and respectful
- Give and accept feedback with care
- Assume good intent and ask when something is unclear
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community
- Help newcomers

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks
- Discriminatory jokes or language
- Sexualized language or unwelcome attention
- Publishing someone else's private information
- Trolling, spam or deliberately disruptive behavior
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any place where someone represents the project in public).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who does so.

To report a problem, use the repository's private **Security** tab reporting option or contact the maintainers through the `Figsh` GitHub profile. All reports are handled privately and in good faith.
